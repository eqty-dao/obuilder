Legal Contract for Digital Asset Ownership of Rolex Fraction

This Agreement

This document (the "Contract") establishes the terms and conditions governing the ownership of a fractional interest in a Rolex watch, specifically 1/1000th ownership, tied to the possession of the digital asset that contains this Contract (the "Digital Asset").

1. Identification of the Rolex Watch

The Rolex watch associated with this Contract is identified by the serial number: ABC123456 (the "Rolex").

2. Ownership Representation

Possession of the Digital Asset containing this Contract signifies ownership of one one-thousandth (1/1000th) of the Rolex. The owner of the Digital Asset ("Holder") is hereby recognized as the rightful fractional owner of the Rolex as defined in this Contract.

3. Transfer of Ownership

Ownership of the 1/1000th fraction of the Rolex is automatically transferred to any party who takes lawful possession of the Digital Asset containing this Contract. Transfer is considered valid upon possession of the Digital Asset by the new party. No separate documentation is required beyond the possession of this Contract within the Digital Asset.

4. Limitations of Ownership

(a) The Holder's ownership is limited to a 1/1000th fractional interest in the Rolex and does not confer any rights to physical possession, usage, or decision-making regarding the Rolex as a whole.

(b) This Contract does not create or imply any fiduciary or managerial relationship between fractional owners or with any custodian of the Rolex.

5. Custody of the Rolex

The Rolex shall remain in the custody of an appointed trustee or custodian unless otherwise agreed upon by all fractional owners. Details regarding the custodian or storage arrangements are not included in this Contract and are subject to separate agreements.

6. Legal Effect

This Contract is binding on the Holder of the Digital Asset and any subsequent transferees. By holding the Digital Asset containing this Contract, the Holder acknowledges and accepts the terms outlined herein.

7. Governing Law

This Contract shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Rolex is physically located at the time of any dispute.

8. Severability

If any provision of this Contract is deemed invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

9. Entire Agreement

This Contract constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the fractional ownership of the Rolex as described herein and supersedes any prior agreements or understandings.

Acknowledgment

By possessing the Digital Asset containing this Contract, the Holder agrees to be bound by the terms herein.

Signed:

No physical signature is required. Possession of this Contract within the Digital Asset serves as acknowledgment and acceptance of its terms.
